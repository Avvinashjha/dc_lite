Given four points `p1`, `p2`, `p3`, and `p4`, each written as `[x, y]`, return whether they are the four vertices of a square.

A square has four equal sides of **positive length** and four right angles. It may be rotated; its sides do not have to be parallel to the coordinate axes.

The points are provided in arbitrary order, not necessarily in order around the boundary. Repeated points, collinear points, non-square rectangles, and rhombuses without right angles are invalid. Return a boolean.
