A company's `n` employees form a tree-shaped management hierarchy, with employee `0` as the head. When the head starts spreading news, each manager needs some time to inform their direct subordinates before the news can continue further down the chain. Find the total time needed for every employee to be informed.

**Example:** n=6, headID=2, manager=[2,2,-1,2,2,2], informTime=[0,0,1,0,0,0] → Output: 1
