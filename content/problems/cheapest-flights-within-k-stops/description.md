There's a flight network of `n` cities connected by one-way flights, where each entry in `flights` is `[from, to, price]`. Starting at city `src`, find the lowest total price to reach city `dst` while making **at most k stops** along the way, and return that price.

**Example:** n=4, flights=[[0,1,100],[1,2,100],[2,0,100],[1,3,600],[2,3,200]], src=0, dst=3, k=1 → Output: 700
