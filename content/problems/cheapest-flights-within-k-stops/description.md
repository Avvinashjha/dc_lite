There's a flight network of `n` cities connected by one-way flights, where each entry in `flights` is `[from, to, price]`. Starting at city `src`, find the lowest total price to reach city `dst` while making **at most k stops** along the way, and return that price. A stop is an intermediate city, so at most `k + 1` flight edges are allowed. Return `-1` when no qualifying route exists.
