Given an `n × n` binary grid containing exactly two islands, return the fewest water cells you must flip from `0` to `1` to connect them.

An island is a maximal group of land cells (`1`s) connected **horizontally or vertically**. Diagonal contact does not connect islands. You may start your bridge from any cell of either island.

Count only flipped water cells, not existing land cells. For `[[0,1],[1,0]]`, flipping either zero connects the two diagonal land cells, so the answer is `1`.
