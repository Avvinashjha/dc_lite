Picture a standard mobile numeric keypad (digits `0` through `9`). Starting from any digit, you may move to the key directly above, below, left, or right of your current position — as long as it stays on the keypad — and press it. Count how many distinct `N`-digit numbers can be typed this way.

**Example:** For `N = 1` there are `10` possible numbers, and for `N = 2` there are `36`.
