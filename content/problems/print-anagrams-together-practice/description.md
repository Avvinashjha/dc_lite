Given an array of lowercase strings `words`, group all anagrams together and return the groups.

Two strings are anagrams when they contain exactly the same characters with the same counts, regardless of order. For example, `"aab"` and `"aba"` are anagrams, but `"abb"` belongs to a different group.

Include every input entry exactly once. Repeated identical words remain repeated entries in their group; do not deduplicate them. Empty strings are anagrams of one another. Use one group per distinct anagram class, and keep words with no anagram partners in singleton groups.

Both group order and the order of words within a group may vary. Return the grouped arrays rather than printing them.
