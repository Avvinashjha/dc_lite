Given a rectangular grid of string characters `"1"` (land) and `"0"` (water), return the number of separate islands.

Land cells belong to the same island when connected through up, down, left, or right steps. Diagonal contact does not connect islands. Treat the area outside the grid as water; land may still touch the grid’s boundary. An island containing a lake remains one island.

Count islands, not land cells. In Example 1, the three connected land cells at the upper left form one island; the bottom-right cell forms another, giving `2`.
