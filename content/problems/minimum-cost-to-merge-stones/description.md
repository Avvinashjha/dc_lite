You have a row of stone piles and a number `k`. In one move, you may merge exactly `k` piles that sit next to each other into a single pile, and that move costs the sum of the stones involved. Keep merging until it's no longer possible, and report the cheapest total cost to reduce the piles that way — or `-1` if they can never all be merged following this rule.

**Example:** With `stones = [3, 2, 4, 1]` and `k = 2`, the minimum total merging cost is `20`.
