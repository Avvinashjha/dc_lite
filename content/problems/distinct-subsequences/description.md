Given strings `s` and `t`, count the ways to select characters at strictly increasing indices in `s` so that they spell `t`. Characters may be skipped, but their order cannot change.

Two selections are distinct when they use different indices, even if the selected characters are identical. For example, `s = "aaa"` and `t = "aa"` has three selections: indices `(0, 1)`, `(0, 2)`, and `(1, 2)`. Matching is case-sensitive.
