Given two nonempty binary trees `root` and `subRoot`, determine whether `subRoot` is a subtree of `root`.

Choose any node of `root` and include **all** of its descendants. This entire tree must match `subRoot` in both values and structure, including which children are missing. You cannot omit extra branches or swap left and right children.

The whole tree may be its own subtree. Repeated values are allowed, so matching one node's value is not sufficient.
