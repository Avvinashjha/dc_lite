You're given `n` non-negative integers describing an elevation map, where each value is the height of a bar one unit wide. After rain falls, figure out how much water ends up trapped between the bars.

Water can escape beyond either end. A position holds water only up to the shorter of the tallest walls to its left and right. Return the total volume, not the largest single gap or a water-level array. Single bars, two bars, and monotonic profiles trap no water.
