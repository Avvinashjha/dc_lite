You have a row of colored boxes given as `boxes`. In each move, pick a maximal run of consecutive boxes that share the same color and remove the entire run at once, earning `k^2` points where `k` is the number of boxes removed in that move. Keep making moves until no boxes remain, and find the highest total score you can achieve.

**Example:** boxes = [1,3,2,2,2,3,4,3,1] → Output: 23
