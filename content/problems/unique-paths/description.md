A robot starts in the top-left cell of a grid with `m` rows and `n` columns. It must reach the bottom-right cell, moving exactly one cell **right** or **down** at each step. There are no blocked cells.

Return the number of distinct paths. Paths are different if their sequences of moves differ. The starting and ending cells are included in every path.

A single row or column has one path. A `1 × 1` grid also has one path: stay in the starting cell without making a move. Inputs are guaranteed to have an answer at most `2 × 10^9`.
