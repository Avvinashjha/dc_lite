# Applied Knowledge Check

This quiz focuses on the trickier parts of the module: choosing refs over state, passing refs through components, and exposing imperative behavior carefully.

Read each question carefully. Refs are powerful, but they should support React's declarative model instead of working around it.

> You need to score **70% or higher** to complete this lesson and finish the module.
> You can retake the quiz as many times as you like - your best score counts.
