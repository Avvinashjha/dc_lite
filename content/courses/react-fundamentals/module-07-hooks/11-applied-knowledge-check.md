# Applied Knowledge Check

This quiz focuses on the trickier parts of the module: choosing the right Hook, managing dependencies, avoiding conditional Hook calls, and understanding memoization tradeoffs.

Read each question carefully. Hook mistakes often appear only after a component re-renders or receives new props.

> You need to score **70% or higher** to complete this lesson and finish the module.
> You can retake the quiz as many times as you like - your best score counts.
