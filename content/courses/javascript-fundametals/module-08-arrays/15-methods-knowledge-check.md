# Array Methods Knowledge Check

You've learned the most important array methods: `forEach`, `map`, `filter`, `reduce`, `find`, `some`, `every`, `slice`, `splice`, `sort`, `reverse`, `flat`, `flatMap`, `includes`, and `entries`.

This quiz checks whether you can choose the right method for a task and predict what each method returns.

> You need to score **70% or higher** to complete this lesson.
> You can retake the quiz as many times as you like - your best score counts.
